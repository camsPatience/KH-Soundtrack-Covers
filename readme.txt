some games have a million variants in this collection, intended to be tagged to specific songs or characters. these uses should hopefully be obvious

"ReMIX Soundtrack" variants are for games/songs that were changed in their remasters. off the top of my head, this applies to KH1, Re:CoM (one of Marluxia's themes was changed iirc), KH2, and Days (five-ish songs were changed).
